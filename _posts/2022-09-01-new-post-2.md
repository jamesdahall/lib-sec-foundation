<h1 class="text-2xl container mx-auto">Thoughts on Security Research Philosophy</h1>

<p class="text-base container mx-auto">At Liberty Security Foundation, we approach research with the belief that knowledge shared is security strengthened. Our work aims to contribute meaningfully to the broader understanding of information security challenges.</p>

<p class="text-base container mx-auto">Independence allows us to explore questions that matter without external constraints. We remain committed to rigorous methodology and transparent communication of our findings.</p>
